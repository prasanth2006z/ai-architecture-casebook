## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out retirement savings for ${company} employees working in ${location}.

## 2. Enrolment
Employees are enrolled automatically ${enrol_days} days after joining and may opt out in writing.

## 3. Contributions
### 3.1 Employer contribution
The company contributes ${employer_percent_g1_6}% of base salary for grade band G1-6 and ${employer_percent_g7_plus}% for grade band G7+.

### 3.2 Employee condition
The employer contribution is paid only while the employee contributes at least ${min_employee_percent}% of base salary.

## 4. Statements
Each employee receives an annual statement through ${portal}.
