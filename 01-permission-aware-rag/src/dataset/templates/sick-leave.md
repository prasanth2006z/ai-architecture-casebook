## 1. Purpose
This policy explains paid sick leave for ${company} employees working in ${location}.

## 2. Sick leave entitlement
Employees may take up to ${sick_days} days of paid sick leave per leave year. The first ${full_pay_days} days are paid at full salary.

## 3. Reporting an absence
Tell your manager by ${report_by}. A medical certificate is required once an absence lasts more than ${cert_days} consecutive days.

## 4. Returning to work
Managers hold a short return-to-work conversation after any absence of more than ${cert_days} days. Questions go to ${service_desk}.
