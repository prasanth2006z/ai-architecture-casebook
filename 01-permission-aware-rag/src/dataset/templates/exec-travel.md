## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out travel rules for senior ${company} executives working in ${location}.

## 2. Scope
It applies to grade band G7+ only. It is restricted and must not be shared with other grade bands.

## 3. Travel class and limits
Flights are booked in ${flight_class}. The hotel limit is ${currency_symbol}${hotel_limit} per night and the car service limit is ${currency_symbol}${car_service_limit} per day.

## 4. Booking
Executives book through the executive travel desk. Standard travel rules in the Business Travel Policy do not apply.
