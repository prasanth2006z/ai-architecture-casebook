## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out annual leave for ${company} employees working in ${location}.

## 2. Scope
It applies to all permanent employees. Contractors follow the terms of their contract.

## 3. Entitlement
### 3.1 Annual leave entitlement
Employees in grade band G1-6 receive ${days_g1_6} days of paid annual leave per leave year. Employees in grade band G7+ receive ${days_g7_plus} days. This is in addition to ${holidays} public holidays.

### 3.2 Eligibility
Leave accrues monthly. An employee with less than ${min_service_months} months of service may only take leave that has already accrued.

## 4. Booking leave
Request leave at least ${notice_days} days in advance through ${portal}. Managers respond within ${response_days} working days.

## 5. Carry-over
Up to ${carry_over_days} unused days may be carried into the next leave year and must be used by ${carry_over_deadline}.
