## 1. Purpose
This policy covers standard relocation support for ${company} employees moving within ${location} at the company's request.

## 2. Scope
It applies to all grade bands. Senior grades are covered by a separate restricted policy.

## 3. Relocation support
### 3.1 Relocation allowance
The company pays a one-time relocation allowance of ${currency_symbol}${allowance} and up to ${temp_housing_weeks} weeks of temporary housing.

### 3.2 Repayment condition
An employee who leaves the company within ${repay_months} months of the move repays the allowance in full.

## 4. Claiming
Submit relocation costs through ${portal} within ${claim_days} days of the move.
