## 1. Purpose
This policy covers paid special leave for ${company} employees working in ${location}.

## 2. Types of special leave
Bereavement leave is ${bereavement_days} days for a close family member. Marriage leave is ${marriage_days} days. Civic duty leave, for example jury service, is ${civic_days} days per year.

## 3. Requesting special leave
Tell your manager at least ${notice_days} days ahead where possible. Bereavement leave may be requested after the event.

## 4. Pay
Special leave is paid at normal salary and does not reduce annual leave.
