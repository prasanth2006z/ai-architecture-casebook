## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how ${company} employees working in ${location} claim business expenses.

## 2. Eligible expenses
Expenses must be reasonable, business-related and covered by another policy, such as travel or meals.

## 3. Claim rules
### 3.1 Claim deadline
Submit each claim through ${portal} within ${claim_days} days of the expense.

### 3.2 Receipts and approval
A receipt is required for any item above ${currency_symbol}${receipt_limit}. A claim above ${currency_symbol}${approval_limit} also needs approval from the department head.

## 4. Payment
Approved claims are paid within ${pay_days} working days.
