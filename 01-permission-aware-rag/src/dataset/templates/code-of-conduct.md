## 1. Purpose
This code sets out the standards of behaviour expected of everyone at ${company}, in ${country_scope}.

## 2. Our standards
We act honestly, respect colleagues, protect company assets and avoid conflicts of interest.

## 3. Raising a concern
Report a concern to your manager or ${service_desk} within ${report_days} working days of becoming aware of it. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not tolerated.

## 4. Training
Every employee completes conduct training every ${training_months} months.
