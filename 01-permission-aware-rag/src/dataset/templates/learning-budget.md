## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the learning budget for ${company} employees working in ${location}.

## 2. Annual budget
Each employee has ${currency_symbol}${annual_budget_g1_6} per year (grade band G1-6) or ${currency_symbol}${annual_budget_g7_plus} per year (grade band G7+) for courses, books and certifications.

## 3. Approval
Request approval through ${portal} at least ${approval_days} working days before enrolling.

## 4. Repayment
An employee who leaves within ${repay_months} months of a course repays its cost.
