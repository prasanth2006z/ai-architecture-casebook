## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out parental leave for ${company} employees working in ${location}.

## 2. Scope
It applies to employees who become a parent by birth. Adoption is covered by the Adoption Leave Policy.

## 3. Entitlement
### 3.1 Parental leave entitlement
An eligible employee may take up to ${weeks} weeks of parental leave per child.

### 3.2 Eligibility
Parental leave is available after ${min_service_months} months of continuous service at the expected start date of the leave.

## 4. Pay
The first ${paid_weeks} weeks are paid at ${pay_percent}% of base salary. Remaining weeks are unpaid.

## 5. Notice
Give ${service_desk} at least ${notice_weeks} weeks of notice before the leave starts.
