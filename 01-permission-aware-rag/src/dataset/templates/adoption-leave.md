## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out adoption leave for ${company} employees working in ${location}.

## 2. Scope
It applies to employees who adopt a child through a recognised adoption agency.

## 3. Entitlement
### 3.1 Adoption leave entitlement
An eligible employee may take up to ${weeks} weeks of adoption leave per child.

### 3.2 Eligibility
Adoption leave is available after ${min_service_months} months of continuous service, and only once the child has been matched with the employee by the adoption agency.

## 4. Pay
The first ${paid_weeks} weeks are paid at ${pay_percent}% of base salary. Remaining weeks are unpaid.

## 5. Notice
Give ${service_desk} at least ${notice_days} days of notice of the placement date.
