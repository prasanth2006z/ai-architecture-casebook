## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out rules for business travel by ${company} employees working in ${location}.

## 2. Scope
It applies to all travel paid by the company, including day trips and overnight stays.

## 3. Travel limits
### 3.1 Flights and hotels
Flights shorter than ${short_haul_hours} hours are booked in economy class. Longer flights may be booked in premium economy. The hotel limit is ${currency_symbol}${hotel_limit_g1_6} per night for grade band G1-6 and ${currency_symbol}${hotel_limit_g7_plus} per night for grade band G7+.

### 3.2 Approval
A trip with an estimated cost above ${currency_symbol}${approval_threshold} needs written approval from your manager at least ${approval_days} working days before booking.

## 4. Booking
Book through ${booking_channel}. Bookings made elsewhere are not reimbursed.
