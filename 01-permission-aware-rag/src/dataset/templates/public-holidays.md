## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out public holidays for ${company} employees working in ${location}.

## 2. Holiday calendar
Employees receive ${holidays} public holidays per year. HR publishes the calendar on ${portal} by the end of ${calendar_month}.

## 3. Working on a public holiday
An employee who is asked to work on a public holiday is paid at ${holiday_rate} times their normal rate or may take time off in lieu, with manager approval.

## 4. Floating days
Each employee also has ${floating_days} floating day(s) per year to use on a day of personal significance.
