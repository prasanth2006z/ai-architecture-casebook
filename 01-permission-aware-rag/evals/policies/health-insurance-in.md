# Health Insurance Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-BN-IN-001 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy describes health insurance for Globex Corp employees working in India.

## 2. Scope
It applies to all permanent employees and their eligible dependants.

## 3. Cover
### 3.1 Insurance cover
The company pays for group hospitalisation insurance for the employee. Cover can be extended to a spouse, up to 2 children and 2 parents. Health check-ups are offered every year for grade band G1-6 and every year for grade band G7+.

### 3.2 Eligibility
Cover starts 0 days after the employee's start date.

## 4. Claims
Contact the Globex HR Service Desk for claim forms and the list of approved providers.
