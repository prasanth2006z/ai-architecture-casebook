# Learning Budget Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-BN-IN-003 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the learning budget for Globex Corp employees working in India.

## 2. Annual budget
Each employee has ₹60000 per year (grade band G1-6) or ₹120000 per year (grade band G7+) for courses, books and certifications.

## 3. Approval
Request approval through the Globex HR portal at least 7 working days before enrolling.

## 4. Repayment
An employee who leaves within 24 months of a course repays its cost.
