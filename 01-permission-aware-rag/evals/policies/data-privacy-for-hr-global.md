# Data Privacy for HR Records

Policy code: HR-GL-003 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how Globex Corp handles personal data in HR records, in all Globex locations.

## 2. Principles
HR records are used only for employment purposes, kept accurate and shared on a need-to-know basis.

## 3. Retention
Employee records are kept for 6 years after employment ends and then deleted.

## 4. Employee requests
An employee may ask the Globex HR Service Desk for a copy of their HR record. The company responds within 30 days.
