# Carers Leave Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-PL-IN-003 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-02-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy covers carers leave for Globex Corp employees working in India.

## 2. Carers leave entitlement
Employees may take up to 7 days of unpaid carers leave per year to look after a dependant with a long-term care need.

## 3. Emergency care
In an emergency, an employee may take up to 3 days of paid leave at short notice.

## 4. Requesting leave
Planned carers leave needs 48 hours of notice through the Globex HR portal.
