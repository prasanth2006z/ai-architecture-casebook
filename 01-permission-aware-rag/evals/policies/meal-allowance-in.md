# Meal Allowance Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-EX-IN-002 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-01-15

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the daily meal allowance for Globex Corp employees working in India while travelling.

## 2. Daily allowance
Employees in grade band G1-6 may claim up to ₹2000 per day. Employees in grade band G7+ may claim up to ₹3000 per day.

## 3. Conditions
The allowance applies to trips of 6 hours or more away from the usual workplace. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## 4. Claiming
Claim meals under the Expense Claims Policy.
