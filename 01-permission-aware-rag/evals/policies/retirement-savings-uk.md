# Retirement Savings Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-BN-UK-002 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out retirement savings for Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Enrolment
Employees are enrolled automatically 90 days after joining and may opt out in writing.

## 3. Contributions
### 3.1 Employer contribution
The company contributes 5% of base salary for grade band G1-6 and 8% for grade band G7+.

### 3.2 Employee condition
The employer contribution is paid only while the employee contributes at least 3% of base salary.

## 4. Statements
Each employee receives an annual statement through the Globex HR portal.
