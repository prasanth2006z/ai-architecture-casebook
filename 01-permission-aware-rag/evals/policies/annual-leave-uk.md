# Annual Leave Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-LV-UK-001 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out annual leave for Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Scope
It applies to all permanent employees. Contractors follow the terms of their contract.

## 3. Entitlement
### 3.1 Annual leave entitlement
Employees in grade band G1-6 receive 25 days of paid annual leave per leave year. Employees in grade band G7+ receive 28 days. This is in addition to 8 public holidays.

### 3.2 Eligibility
Leave accrues monthly. An employee with less than 3 months of service may only take leave that has already accrued.

## 4. Booking leave
Request leave at least 14 days in advance through the Globex HR portal. Managers respond within 3 working days.

## 5. Carry-over
Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next leave year and must be used by 31 March.
