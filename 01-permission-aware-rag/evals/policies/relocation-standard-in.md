# Standard Relocation Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-TR-IN-003 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy covers standard relocation support for Globex Corp employees moving within India at the company's request.

## 2. Scope
It applies to all grade bands. Senior grades are covered by a separate restricted policy.

## 3. Relocation support
### 3.1 Relocation allowance
The company pays a one-time relocation allowance of ₹300000 and up to 3 weeks of temporary housing.

### 3.2 Repayment condition
An employee who leaves the company within 18 months of the move repays the allowance in full.

## 4. Claiming
Submit relocation costs through the Globex HR portal within 45 days of the move.
