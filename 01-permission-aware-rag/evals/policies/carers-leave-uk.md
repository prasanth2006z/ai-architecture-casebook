# Carers Leave Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-PL-UK-003 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-02-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy covers carers leave for Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Carers leave entitlement
Employees may take up to 5 days of unpaid carers leave per year to look after a dependant with a long-term care need.

## 3. Emergency care
In an emergency, an employee may take up to 2 days of paid leave at short notice.

## 4. Requesting leave
Planned carers leave needs 24 hours of notice through the Globex HR portal.
