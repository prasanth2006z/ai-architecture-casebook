# Parental Leave Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-PL-UK-001-V1 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2025-07-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out parental leave for Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Scope
It applies to employees who become a parent by birth. Adoption is covered by the Adoption Leave Policy.

## 3. Entitlement
### 3.1 Parental leave entitlement
An eligible employee may take up to 18 weeks of parental leave per child.

### 3.2 Eligibility
Parental leave is available after 6 months of continuous service at the expected start date of the leave.

## 4. Pay
The first 12 weeks are paid at 80% of base salary. Remaining weeks are unpaid.

## 5. Notice
Give the Globex HR Service Desk at least 15 weeks of notice before the leave starts.
