# Health Insurance Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-BN-UK-001 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy describes health insurance for Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Scope
It applies to all permanent employees and their eligible dependants.

## 3. Cover
### 3.1 Insurance cover
The company pays for private medical insurance for the employee. Cover can be extended to a spouse or partner and up to 2 children. Health check-ups are offered every 2 years for grade band G1-6 and every year for grade band G7+.

### 3.2 Eligibility
Cover starts 30 days after the employee's start date.

## 4. Claims
Contact the Globex HR Service Desk for claim forms and the list of approved providers.
