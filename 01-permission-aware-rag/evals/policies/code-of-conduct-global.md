# Code of Conduct

Policy code: HR-GL-001 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose
This code sets out the standards of behaviour expected of everyone at Globex Corp, in all Globex locations.

## 2. Our standards
We act honestly, respect colleagues, protect company assets and avoid conflicts of interest.

## 3. Raising a concern
Report a concern to your manager or the Globex HR Service Desk within 5 working days of becoming aware of it. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not tolerated.

## 4. Training
Every employee completes conduct training every 12 months.
