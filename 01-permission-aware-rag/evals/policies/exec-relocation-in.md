# Executive Relocation Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-XE-IN-001 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-06-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out relocation support for senior Globex Corp executives moving to or within India.

## 2. Scope
It applies to grade band G7+ only. It is restricted and must not be shared with other grade bands.

## 3. Relocation package
The package includes a relocation allowance of ₹2500000, 4 months of company-paid housing, 2 family flights and tax advice for 2 years.

## 4. Approval
The package is approved by the executive committee and confirmed in writing by the Globex HR Service Desk.
