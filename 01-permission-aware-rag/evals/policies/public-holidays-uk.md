# Public Holidays Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-LV-UK-003 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out public holidays for Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Holiday calendar
Employees receive 8 public holidays per year. HR publishes the calendar on the Globex HR portal by the end of November.

## 3. Working on a public holiday
An employee who is asked to work on a public holiday is paid at 1.5 times their normal rate or may take time off in lieu, with manager approval.

## 4. Floating days
Each employee also has 1 floating day(s) per year to use on a day of personal significance.
