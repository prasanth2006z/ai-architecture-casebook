# Expense Claims Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-EX-UK-001 · Version 2.0 · Effective 2026-07-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom claim business expenses.

## 2. Eligible expenses
Expenses must be reasonable, business-related and covered by another policy, such as travel or meals.

## 3. Claim rules
### 3.1 Claim deadline
Submit each claim through the Globex HR portal within 30 days of the expense.

### 3.2 Receipts and approval
A receipt is required for any item above £25. A claim above £500 also needs approval from the department head.

## 4. Payment
Approved claims are paid within 10 working days.
