# Executive Relocation Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-XE-UK-001 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-06-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out relocation support for senior Globex Corp executives moving to or within the United Kingdom.

## 2. Scope
It applies to grade band G7+ only. It is restricted and must not be shared with other grade bands.

## 3. Relocation package
The package includes a relocation allowance of £40000, 6 months of company-paid housing, 4 family flights and tax advice for 2 years.

## 4. Approval
The package is approved by the executive committee and confirmed in writing by the Globex HR Service Desk.
