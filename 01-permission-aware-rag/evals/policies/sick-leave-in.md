# Sick Leave Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-LV-IN-002 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains paid sick leave for Globex Corp employees working in India.

## 2. Sick leave entitlement
Employees may take up to 12 days of paid sick leave per leave year. The first 12 days are paid at full salary.

## 3. Reporting an absence
Tell your manager by 09:30 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required once an absence lasts more than 3 consecutive days.

## 4. Returning to work
Managers hold a short return-to-work conversation after any absence of more than 3 days. Questions go to the Globex HR Service Desk.
