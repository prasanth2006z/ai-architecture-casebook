# Home Office Equipment Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-RW-UK-002 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-05-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy covers equipment for home working by Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Equipment allowance
Employees with a remote work arrangement receive a one-time allowance of £400 for a desk, chair and accessories.

## 3. Refresh
Equipment may be refreshed every 4 years. A desk assessment is offered on request.

## 4. Ownership
Equipment bought with the allowance belongs to the employee. Company laptops remain company property.
