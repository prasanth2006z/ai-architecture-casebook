# Sick Leave Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-LV-UK-002 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains paid sick leave for Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Sick leave entitlement
Employees may take up to 10 days of paid sick leave per leave year. The first 5 days are paid at full salary.

## 3. Reporting an absence
Tell your manager by 10:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required once an absence lasts more than 7 consecutive days.

## 4. Returning to work
Managers hold a short return-to-work conversation after any absence of more than 7 days. Questions go to the Globex HR Service Desk.
