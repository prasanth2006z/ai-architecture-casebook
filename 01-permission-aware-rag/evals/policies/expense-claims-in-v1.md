# Expense Claims Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-EX-IN-001-V1 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2025-07-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how Globex Corp employees working in India claim business expenses.

## 2. Eligible expenses
Expenses must be reasonable, business-related and covered by another policy, such as travel or meals.

## 3. Claim rules
### 3.1 Claim deadline
Submit each claim through the Globex HR portal within 30 days of the expense.

### 3.2 Receipts and approval
A receipt is required for any item above ₹500. A claim above ₹25000 also needs approval from the department head.

## 4. Payment
Approved claims are paid within 7 working days.
