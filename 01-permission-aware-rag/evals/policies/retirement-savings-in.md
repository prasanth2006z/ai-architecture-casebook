# Retirement Savings Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-BN-IN-002 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out retirement savings for Globex Corp employees working in India.

## 2. Enrolment
Employees are enrolled automatically 30 days after joining and may opt out in writing.

## 3. Contributions
### 3.1 Employer contribution
The company contributes 12% of base salary for grade band G1-6 and 12% for grade band G7+.

### 3.2 Employee condition
The employer contribution is paid only while the employee contributes at least 12% of base salary.

## 4. Statements
Each employee receives an annual statement through the Globex HR portal.
