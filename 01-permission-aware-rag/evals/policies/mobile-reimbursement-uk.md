# Mobile Phone Reimbursement Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-EX-UK-003 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-01-15

## 1. Purpose
This policy covers reimbursement of personal mobile phone costs for Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Monthly limit
The company reimburses up to £30 per month of mobile plan costs.

## 3. Business use
At least 30% of usage must be for business. HR may ask for a usage summary.

## 4. Review
The limit is reviewed every 12 months.
