# Anti-Harassment Policy

Policy code: HR-GL-002 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out how Globex Corp prevents and handles harassment, in all Globex locations.

## 2. What is not acceptable
Harassment, bullying and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated at work or at work events.

## 3. Reporting and investigation
the Globex HR Service Desk acknowledges a report within 2 working days and aims to finish the investigation within 30 days.

## 4. Support
Anyone who raises a concern is offered confidential support.
