# Travel Booking Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-TR-IN-002 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-03-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how Globex Corp employees working in India book travel.

## 2. Booking in advance
Book at least 7 days before departure. Late bookings need manager approval.

## 3. Changes and cancellations
Cancel at least 24 hours before departure to avoid a penalty. Change fees above ₹8000 need manager approval.

## 4. Rail travel
Rail journeys are booked in air-conditioned second class.
