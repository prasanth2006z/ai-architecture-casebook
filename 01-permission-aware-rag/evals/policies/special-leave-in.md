# Special Leave Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-LV-IN-004 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy covers paid special leave for Globex Corp employees working in India.

## 2. Types of special leave
Bereavement leave is 3 days for a close family member. Marriage leave is 5 days. Civic duty leave, for example jury service, is 1 days per year.

## 3. Requesting special leave
Tell your manager at least 10 days ahead where possible. Bereavement leave may be requested after the event.

## 4. Pay
Special leave is paid at normal salary and does not reduce annual leave.
