# Learning Budget Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-BN-UK-003 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the learning budget for Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Annual budget
Each employee has £750 per year (grade band G1-6) or £1500 per year (grade band G7+) for courses, books and certifications.

## 3. Approval
Request approval through the Globex HR portal at least 10 working days before enrolling.

## 4. Repayment
An employee who leaves within 12 months of a course repays its cost.
