# Remote Work Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-RW-IN-001 · Version 2.0 · Effective 2026-07-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out remote work for Globex Corp employees working in India.

## 2. Scope
It applies to roles that do not need daily presence on site. Managers decide which roles qualify.

## 3. Remote work arrangements
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Request an arrangement through the Globex HR portal at least 15 days before it starts.

## 4. Availability and security
Be reachable during core hours, 11:00 to 16:00. Use only company-approved devices and the company VPN for work systems.

## 5. Additional notes
Managers review each remote arrangement once a year.
