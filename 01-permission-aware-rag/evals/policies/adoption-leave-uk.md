# Adoption Leave Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-PL-UK-002 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-02-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out adoption leave for Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Scope
It applies to employees who adopt a child through a recognised adoption agency.

## 3. Entitlement
### 3.1 Adoption leave entitlement
An eligible employee may take up to 20 weeks of adoption leave per child.

### 3.2 Eligibility
Adoption leave is available after 9 months of continuous service, and only once the child has been matched with the employee by the adoption agency.

## 4. Pay
The first 12 weeks are paid at 80% of base salary. Remaining weeks are unpaid.

## 5. Notice
Give the Globex HR Service Desk at least 28 days of notice of the placement date.
