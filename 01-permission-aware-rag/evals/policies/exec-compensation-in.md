# Executive Compensation Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-XE-IN-002 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-06-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out compensation principles for senior Globex Corp executives working in India.

## 2. Scope
It applies to grade band G7+ only. It is restricted and must not be shared with other grade bands.

## 3. Variable pay
The annual bonus target is 35% of base salary. Long-term incentive awards vest over 3 years.

## 4. Review and clawback
Compensation is reviewed each April. Variable pay may be reclaimed within 18 months in case of a restatement or misconduct.
