# Remote Work Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-RW-UK-001 · Version 2.0 · Effective 2026-07-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out remote work for Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Scope
It applies to roles that do not need daily presence on site. Managers decide which roles qualify.

## 3. Remote work arrangements
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Request an arrangement through the Globex HR portal at least 10 days before it starts.

## 4. Availability and security
Be reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00. Use only company-approved devices and the company VPN for work systems.

## 5. Additional notes
Managers review each remote arrangement once a year.
