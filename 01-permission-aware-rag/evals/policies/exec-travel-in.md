# Executive Travel Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-XE-IN-003 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-06-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out travel rules for senior Globex Corp executives working in India.

## 2. Scope
It applies to grade band G7+ only. It is restricted and must not be shared with other grade bands.

## 3. Travel class and limits
Flights are booked in business class. The hotel limit is ₹30000 per night and the car service limit is ₹12000 per day.

## 4. Booking
Executives book through the executive travel desk. Standard travel rules in the Business Travel Policy do not apply.
