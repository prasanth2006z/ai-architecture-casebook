# Public Holidays Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-LV-IN-003 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out public holidays for Globex Corp employees working in India.

## 2. Holiday calendar
Employees receive 10 public holidays per year. HR publishes the calendar on the Globex HR portal by the end of December.

## 3. Working on a public holiday
An employee who is asked to work on a public holiday is paid at 2 times their normal rate or may take time off in lieu, with manager approval.

## 4. Floating days
Each employee also has 2 floating day(s) per year to use on a day of personal significance.
