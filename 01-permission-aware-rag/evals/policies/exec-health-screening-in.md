# Executive Health Screening Policy (India)

Policy code: HR-XE-IN-004 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-06-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy covers health screening for senior Globex Corp executives working in India.

## 2. Scope
It applies to grade band G7+ only. It is restricted and must not be shared with other grade bands.

## 3. Screening benefit
The company pays up to ₹120000 for a full health screening every 12 months. Screening is carried out by a private hospital chosen from the approved list.

## 4. Confidentiality
Results go to the executive only. The company receives confirmation of attendance.
