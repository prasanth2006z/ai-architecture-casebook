# Business Travel Policy (United Kingdom)

Policy code: HR-TR-UK-001-V1 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2025-07-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out rules for business travel by Globex Corp employees working in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Scope
It applies to all travel paid by the company, including day trips and overnight stays.

## 3. Travel limits
### 3.1 Flights and hotels
Flights shorter than 4 hours are booked in economy class. Longer flights may be booked in premium economy. The hotel limit is £120 per night for grade band G1-6 and £180 per night for grade band G7+.

### 3.2 Approval
A trip with an estimated cost above £1000 needs written approval from your manager at least 5 working days before booking.

## 4. Booking
Book through the Globex travel desk. Bookings made elsewhere are not reimbursed.
